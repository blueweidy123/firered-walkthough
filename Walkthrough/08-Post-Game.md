## POST-GAME CONTENT AND BATTLES

Now that we've completed all eight regions, there are a TON of rematches that become available. You can find about most of the fights by reading books on the desk in Oak's Lab.

But first, a number of items used to change the forms of legendary Pokémon have popped up around the world, so let’s grab them for ourselves. Note that you will have needed to capture/defeat the legendary Pokémon first, for the item to appear.
Item Quest:
Red Orb and Blue Orb - Route 124, where Groudon and Kyogre were. Used to change Groudon and Kyogre into their respective Primal forms.
DNA Splicers - Icirrus Cave, where Kyurem was. Used to fuse Kyurem with Reshiram or Zekrom.
Zygarde Cube - Terminus Cave, where Zygarde was. Used to change Zygarde’s form and ability (no Zygarde Cells required, fortunately).
Ultranecrozium Z - Ultra Deep Space, where Lusamine was. Used to change Dusk Mane Necrozma or Dawn Wings Necrozma into Ultra Necrozma in battle and use its exclusive Z-move, Light that Burns the Sky.
Reins of Unity - Freezington, where Calyrex was. Used to allow Calyrex to ride on (fuse with) Glastrier or Spectrier.
??? - Talking to Oak with your dex completed! Used to allow *r***s to change forms in battle.
